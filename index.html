About

I am a master’s student in the School of Microelectronics at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), studying Electronic Science and Technology. I study KV cache compression for long-context LLM inference and low-bit model quantization.

I am currently an AI infrastructure intern in Tencent TEG’s Data Computing Platform Department, working on attention kernels and inference systems. From August 2025 to June 2026, I was a visiting research student at Nanyang Technological University in Singapore.

EMNLP 2026 · Main Conference · Oral2026

SlimKV: Joint Token-Feature KV Cache Compression with Reconstruction-Free Beacon Attention

Zihan Teng, Jiayu Zhao, Wentao Ren, Minhao Fan, Tianrui Ma, Song Chen, Weichen Liu

SlimKV compresses both the token and feature dimensions of the KV cache, then computes attention directly in the compressed latent space. In experiments with a 128K context, it achieves up to 7.34× attention speedup and 3.38× end-to-end decoding speedup.

BitsMoE: Cost-Aware Bit Allocation in Spectral Space for MoE LLM Quantization

Jiayu Zhao, Zihan Teng, Minhao Fan, Tianrui Ma, Wentao Ren, Song Chen, Weichen Liu

BitsMoE decomposes MoE expert weights into spectral components and allocates bit widths according to quantization cost under a memory budget. I helped refine the optimization objective and cross-layer allocation, reproduced baselines, and evaluated low-bit quantization performance.

Other work

Long-context compression

I used cross-layer SVD to replace the KV cache with low-rank hidden states. Precomputing the bases reduced the cost of compression during inference.

Inference systems & kernels

I develop FP8 attention backends for vLLM using CUDA and FlashAttention-3/CUTLASS. I also optimize multimodal inference pipelines and build tools to trace numerical errors.
